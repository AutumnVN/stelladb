# Nazuna

Nazuna takes on the Main slot as a buffer for this team, providing high amounts of ATK% buff with **Fill the Gap** + **Cutie's Rally** and Skill Crit Rate with **Operation: Pandemonium**.

## Skill

1/1/1/1 (Not needed)

None of Nazuna's skills benefit this team as they are all Terra-aligned so don't bother leveling any of them for this team.

# Freesia

***As of Patch 1.9, Ice Vortex has been changed to only stack one time per enemy instead of three. So this build is not as good as it was pre-patch.**

Freesia's DPS build revolves around the **Localized Snowfall** potential. This potential enables her Auto Attacks to have a 30% chance to spawn Ice Flowers within 6s after using her Support Skill in Berserk state.

**★ Key Notes:**
• **Ice Vortex** provides a significant amount of Crit DMG buff that scales dynamically based on the number of enemies hit on the field.
• **Penetrating Chill** and **Winter's Grip** stacks up faster when she is near the enemy.
• **Blessing of Faith** is only taken for its 3s Berserk extension effect.
• **Power of Redemption** is only taken if you have a surplus for potentials since she is not able to fully utilize it.

## Skill

1/1/10/1 (Support Skill only)

@skill(Frost Bloom) Freesia's damage output is fully reliant on **Frost Bloom (Support Skill)** since this is where the scaling of **Localized Snowfall** depends on so make sure to upgrade it as much as you can.

### Priority

# Tilia

Tilia acts as the baseline buffer for this team as it is easier to make a Record with her since she only needs two potentials in order to provide high ATK% buffs.

## Skill

1/1/10/1 (Support Skill only)

@skill(Aegis Intervention) **Aegis Intervention (Support Skill)** provides an ATK% buff to the team when her skill deals DMG. This buff scales according to the level of the skill and thus, it is beneficial to upgrade it.

# Iris

Iris is a better option as the 2nd Support slot, providing a higher ATK% buff plus an Aqua DMG Taken debuff from her potentials.

**★ Key Notes:**
• **Blizzard: Stack** and **Blizzard: Impact** are taken in order for **Bundle Sale** to hit more reliably against bosses that spawn additional enemies.

## Skill

1/1/1/1 (Not needed)

Iris is only used for her valuable Potential kit and none of her skills scale buffs or debuffs so there is no need to waste resources on her.
