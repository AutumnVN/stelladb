# Canace

Canace is a cheap option to place on the Main slot for this team as she does not need any investments at all to function within the team. Her only purpose is to provide Ventus DMG Taken debuff through her Ventus-imbued Auto Attack bullets along with a slight ATK% buff from Subject Boost . ★ Key Notes: • Most Admired is important for Subject Boost as it increases the ATK% buff that it provides. • Erratic Weather is important for Weakness Analysis as it provides more chances of firing Ventus-imbued bullets. • Both Comprehensive Praise and Shadow Stride are nice to have but not really needed.

## Skill

Canace is only used to fill in the Main slot for Nazuka and provide Ventus-related buffs/debuffs through her potentials and thus, there is no need to invest in her skills at all.

# Ann

Ann provides ample amounts of ATK% buff and Ventus DMG taken debuffs through her Support Skill and potentials. ★ Key Notes: • Forest Princess' Blessing is a critical hit in essence since it provides a flat 35% chance to increase the Ventus DMG dealt by an ally.
