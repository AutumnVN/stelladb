# Chixia

Chixia is able to provide high amounts of buffs for Snowish Laru, albeit it takes time to build-up.

**★ Key Notes:**
• **Horn Call** and **Barrier Construct** scales based on the amount of Chixia's minions in the field.
• **Prayer of Raging Flame** relies on hitting multiple targets to stack up the ATK% buff it provides. It will never stack if you are only hitting one target.
