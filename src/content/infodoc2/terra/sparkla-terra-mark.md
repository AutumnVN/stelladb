# Nazuna

Nazuna remains to be the primary Mark DMG buffer primarily with **Touch of Nature** and several sources of potent buffs and debuffs.

**★ Key Notes:**
• Nazuna has to keep moving so she can regenerate Energy faster.
• **Touch of Nature** is her most important potential. If you don't have it maxed, reset.
• **Small Force** and **Wobble Dance** lets her deployed penguin stack Terra Marks and Terra DMG Taken at the same time.
• **Rocket Cart** provides a lot of value due to its potential in inflicting high amounts of Terra DMG Taken debuff.
• **Swiftstep** is mainly taken if you need the additional dodge.
• **Balloonado** is only considered for challenging Menace Arena.

## Skill

1/10/1/10 (Main Skill > Ultimate)

@skill(Plushie Dance) **Plushie Dance (Main Skill)** is the primary priority as it provides Terra DMG Taken debuff to enemies, which scales based on the skill's level.

@skill(Dream Spiral) **Dream Spiral (Ultimate)** is the last priority providing a high ATK% buff that scales based on the skill's level.

# Springseek Coronis

Springseek Coronis' role is to provide Mark DMG Taken debuff plus additional ATK% buffs and Terra DMG Taken debuffs.

**★ Key Notes:**
• **Terra Chorus** is her most important potential. If you don't have this, reset.
• **Lasting Aftertaste** allows her vials to be in-line with Gerie's on-field time.
• Ensure that enemies are within her vials range otherwise, her contribution drops heavily.

## Skill

1/1/1/1 (Not needed)

None of her skills scale buffs or debuffs and thus, it is not worth it to waste resources for her in this team.
