# Nazuna

Nazuna is able to provide high amounts of buffs and debuffs through her skills and potentials, allowing her to be the core support unit for the team. ★ Key Notes: • The combination of Small Force and Plushie Dance (Main Skill) allows Nazuna to inflict huge amounts of Terra DMG Taken debuff. • Take note of when you should dodge (usually before S. Coronis uses her skill) so you don't miss out on Swiftstep 's ATK buff.

## Skill

Plushie Dance (Main Skill) is the primary priority as it provides Terra DMG Taken debuff to enemies, which scales based on the skill's level. Dream Spiral (Ultimate) is the next priority as it provides the team an ATK% buff that scales based on the skill's level.

# Springseek Coronis

S. Coronis' Rose Vial build allows you to speedrun because she can dish out a devastating amount of burst damage thanks to her potentials and her teammates' fast acting buffs + debuffs combined. This build is so good to the point where at high investments , she only needs two Support Skill rotations to clear Finale Echoing Beyond difficulty . ★ Key Notes: • This build is specifically designed for Finale Echoing speedruns. It is NOT as effective in other game modes.

## Skill

Instant Blossom (Support Skill) is the only source of damage for S. Coronis in this speedrun team. Essence Extraction 's insane damage multiplier depends heavily on its skill level so make sure that it is maxed.

# Tilia

Tilia's role for the team is to provide high amounts of buffs through her Support Skill and potentials. There isn't much options that can be taken for her in this case since it's a speedrun team. The team performs whether you choose to use Ridge or Tilia. ★ Key Notes: • Perfect Spiral provides Skill DMG Taken debuffs which can help in softening the boss. • Trick Boost and Revealed Flaw are her generic ATK buffing potentials.

## Skill

Aegis Intervention (Support Skill) provides an ATK% buff to the team when her skill deals damage. This buff scales according to the level of the skill and thus, it is beneficial to upgrade it.

# Ridge

Ridge's role for the team is to contribute a heavy amount of DEF Shred debuff with Corrosive Vines and Root Grasp along with some ATK% buffs with Small Changes . The team performs whether you choose to use Ridge or Tilia. ★ Key Notes: • S. Coronis gains an additional 67% Skill DMG if you use Ridge instead of Tilia because of Vernal Breath . • Reblossom recasts her Support Skill, which helps in stacking up the DEF Shred debuff from Corrosive Vines .

## Skill

Ridge is only used for her Potential kit and none of her skills scale buffs or debuffs so there is no need to waste resources on her.
