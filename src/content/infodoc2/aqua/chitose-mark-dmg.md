# Suntide Willow

*Currently within feelscrafting territory. Be warned. Suntide Willow provides Mark DMG Taken and some other Mark related stuff (will do full write up later) ★ Key Notes: •

## Skill

Prioritize upgrading Frolic in the Sea (Support Skill) first and Beach Warning (Ultimate) next since her Support Skill gives more buffs than her Ultimate. Unfortunately, the buff scaling for both skills is a bit underwhelming compared to other Trekkers. Additionally, while her Support Skill does affect the damage scaling of Flying Droplets and Flooding Shore , her damage contribution remains to be minimal. Therefore, you can comfortably stop at Level 7 if you're low on resources.

# Tilia

Of course Tilia is involved yet again when Mark DMG is involved thanks to Blinding Beam . ★ Key Notes: • Blinding Beam is her most important potential for this team. If you don't have it maxed, reset . • Holy Inscription enables the Mark DMG Taken debuff from Blinding Beam to be consistently active as long as the enemy is within the circle it spawns. • Renewed Spectrum is taken to reduce the cooldown of her Support Skill.

## Skill

Aegis Intervention (Support Skill) provides an ATK% buff to the team when her skill deals damage. This buff scales according to the level of the skill and thus, it is beneficial to upgrade it.
