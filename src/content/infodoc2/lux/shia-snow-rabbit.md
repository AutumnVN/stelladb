# Shia

Shia's Snow Rabbit build involves taking advantage of the Auto Attack chain reset mechanic by doing Dodge → Hold (until you see Snow Rabbit drones behind her) → Auto Attack repeatedly. ★ Key Notes: • Hare's Pursuit is a very good potential that will allow you to spam the dash cancel technique even faster than without it , allowing for more dash cancels in a given period of time. • If you dash cancel precisely after Shia uses her Main Skill, you can skip the delay in her skill's cooldown. • Sticking as close as possible to the enemy is the name of the game. The goal is to shotgun the enemy at point blank to make sure that all of the bullets hit.

## Skill

Sail of Twin Moons (Auto Attack) will be the main priority as most of Shia's damage output lies on it, especially with the dash cancel tech. Phantom Step Radiance and Rimecrystal Edge scales based on the level of Whirling Waves (Main Skill) . Using Divine Deluge (Ultimate) usually results in a slight DPS loss since it is much better to just spam Shia's AA1, leading to it being entirely ignored or used in situations where you need the i-frame it provides while still dealing damage.

# Tilia

*Currently in re-evaluation phase. Tilia is the premier must-have support for Lux teams as she provides Lux DMG Taken debuffs plus ATK% and Mark DMG% buffs along with a consistent Lux Mark application. ★ Key Notes: • Blinding Beam becomes an optional potential if: 1) you can speedrun the boss faster than 16s which makes it so that the accumulated DMG overtime of Lux Mark triggers are negligible and 2) you can't keep the boss within Holy Inscription 's circle due to its mobility. • Perfect Spiral is preferred as not only does it provide a Skill DMG Taken debuff but also because the cooldown reduction from Renewed Spectrum is somewhat redundant due to her buffs having a near 100% uptime. Future Relevant Notes (WIP): • According to (it's timestamped), Trick Boost refreshes its duration when Tilia leaves the field for some reason. This means that it actually has better uptime and has perfect synergy with Renewed Spectrum .

## Skill

Aegis Intervention (Support Skill) provides an ATK% buff to the team when her skill deals DMG. This buff scales according to the level of the skill and thus, it is beneficial to upgrade it.
