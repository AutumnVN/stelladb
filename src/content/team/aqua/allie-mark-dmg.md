# Allie

Allie's Mark build relies on the conversion of her AA DMG and Ultimate DMG into Mark DMG via **Wide Blade Arc**. The typical rotation is AA → wait until Main Skill lights up → Main Skill (Ult Phase 1) and if her Ultimate skill is available, use it ASAP (after Main Skill's Ult Phase 1) then ignore its 2nd phase.

Her Mark trigger effect, Tide, deals Mark DMG and contains two effects: Flood and Ebb. Flood does nothing on its own but it can stack up to 9 times to generate Ebb, dealing Mark DMG to the target and resetting all Flood stacks. With the **Sweeping Extension** potential, she is able to generate Ebb for every 3 stacks of Flood, with the 3rd Ebb becoming Abyssal Wave.

The "Wave" seen in the description of **Sweeping Extension** and **Pending Chore** appears to be a mistranslation of Ebb.

**★ Key Notes:**
• It is safe to use this team in Auto Battle because she only uses her Main Skill if it is lit up.
• **Wide Blade Arc** prevents Mark DMG from doing critical hits, hence Crit Rate and Crit DMG is irrelevant in this build.
• If you are able to get 260% AA DMG, then you can max out **Wide Blade Arc**.
• **Pending Chore** needs to at least be Lv 4 to gain stacks for every 1 time Ebb is generated by Flood.
• **Overtime Chore** enhances her AA skill to have a higher damage multiplier and allows it to trigger Marks.
• **Swift Wipe** provides an enormous boost to her Ultimate Phase 1's damage multiplier. It is better than **Overtime Chore** in emblem priority if her Ultimate level is 13.

## Skill

@skill(Deep Sweep) @skill(Housework Slash) Since we are able to use her **Deep Sweep: Dust Off (Ultimate Phase 1)** consistently via **Sweeping Rush (Main Skill)** and her actual Ultimate skill, raising her Ultimate sees better value to prioritize than **Housework Slash (Auto Attack)**.

None of her potentials scale on any of her skills (**Sweeping Extension** scales based on Trekker tier) so it's a non-factor in this case.

# Suntide Willow

Suntide Willow is the core support of the team, providing a wide array of buffs and debuffs that are tailored towards the Mark build.

**★ Key Notes:**
• **Hovering Gale** and **Words Unchanged** are her most important potentials for this team, ensure they're maxed.
• **Words Unchanged** increases the cap of Smiling Sanction from 6 to 12 stacks at max level, increasing the Mark DMG Taken debuff and additionally inflicting Aqua DMG Taken per stack to the enemy.
• **Engulfing Tide** expands her skill's radius further while giving Aqua DMG buff for the team.
• **Marine Justice** inflicts a good amount of Aqua DMG Taken debuff, and having 40% CE is usually enough to proc it consistently.

## Skill

1/1/10/10 (Support Skill > Ultimate)

@skill(Frolic in the Sea) @skill(Beach Warning) Prioritize upgrading **Frolic in the Sea (Support Skill)** first and **Beach Warning (Ultimate)** next since her Support Skill gives more buffs than her Ultimate.

You can comfortably stop at Level 7 if you're low on resources since her buff scaling is rather underwhelming compared to others.

# Teresa

Teresa is better than Donna for this particular team as she is able to boost Allie's ATK SPD with **Isn't it Awesome** by 25% and provide better buffs plus a huge debuff from **Wave Breaker**.

**★ Key Notes:**
• **Isn't it Awesome** is fully reliant on having the shield intact for both the ATK SPD and ATK% buff to work, do not lose the shield.
• For this team, having 40% CE on her emblems is enough to cast Ultimate 5 times, allowing **Wave Breaker** even more potent.

# Donna - Sub-DPS Build

Donna's use-case in this team is to be a sub-DPS using her usual DPS build. She does not benefit much from the buffs given by Suntide Willow since she is a Skill DMG Trekker.

Do take note that you will want to have a built Donna in the first place if you want her to be a sub-DPS for this team.

**★ Key Notes:**
• You need 1 level of **Chilling Onslaught** for **Aquatic Assault** to function.
• Alternatively, you can use **Messenger Arrow** instead if you're struggling to get both **Aquatic Assault** and **Chilling Onslaught**.
• If you're using **Messenger Arrow**, make sure that Allie actually picks up the letter.
