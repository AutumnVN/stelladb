# Chaton

Chaton's Dark Ray build is executed by tapping her Main Skill to activate Dark Ray, shooting a laser and inflicting Dark Mark on targets hit. This effect deals Skill DMG every 2 seconds, which can be cut down in half with **Meowth's Curse** potential.

**★ Key Notes:**
• Try to use her skill when she has the most buffs since Dark Mark snapshots Effect type buffs upon application.
• If the stage allows it, you can pre-stack **The Cat's Treasure** and/or **Joyful Gathering**'s buff by using her skill at least once before engaging in battle.
• If you are using **The Cat's Treasure** as a Main Disc, only use her Ultimate if: 1) Main Skill is at 6s cooldown or 2) **Lucky Bullet** resets her skill to prevent losing its stacks.
• **Lucky Bullet** also affects the Skill DMG dealt by **Mark of Darkness**.
• Having at least one level of **Dominant Firepower** enables her to quickly charge everyone's Ultimate.

@skill(Full-Auto Trigger) @skill(Dark Evil Eye) Both **Full-Auto Trigger (Auto Attack)** and **Dark Evil Eye (Ultimate)** can be optionally upgraded as well but not as much as her Main Skill, preferably only until Level 7 to help against bosses that summon additional targets.

# Flora

Flora is currently the irreplaceable support of this team. The massive buff and debuff utility that she can offer for the team is currently incomparable with other options.

**★ Key Notes:**
• Regarding Charge Efficiency, getting to 30% CE is probably enough.
• **Ashes of the Past** offers a better result if the team has little to no Crit Rate.
• Alternatively, **Ember of Tomorrow** will be used if your Crit Rate is already high.
• **Ember Rekindled** can enable a permanent uptime for **Soulflare Butterfly**'s debuff assuming that the target has **Butterfly Mark**.
• **Foreshadowing Verification**'s huge boost in Crit DMG is the main reason why Ignis teams typically rely on critting
• **Reinforced Impression** offers a lot of Crit Rate gain especially when paired with **Ashes of the Past**.
• Keep enemies inside her Ultimate to build up **Lethal Inferno**'s debuff stacks and to maintain its uptime.

## Skill

1/1/10/1 (Support Skill only)

@skill(Flutter Flare) Only upgrade **Flutter Flare (Support Skill)** as it increases the Crit DMG given by Mindflare Butterfly. Keep in mind that the Crit DMG buff does not correlate with the amount of Mindflare Butterflies present.

# Snowish Laru

Snowish Laru is able to provide instantaneous high amounts of ATK% buffs and Ignis DMG Taken debuff from her potentials.

**★ Key Notes:**
• Regarding Charge Efficiency, getting to 30% CE is probably enough.
• **Fairy Tale Rule** provides a huge amount of Ignis DMG Taken debuff but only if the team is composed of Ignis Trekkers.
• **Two-Gun Salute** extends her Support Skill's uptime.
• **Heart's Benediction** is only taken if you have Snowy Night Surprise disc. Do not consider taking it otherwise.

## Skill

# Tilia

If you do not have Snowish Laru, Tilia can take her place as a hyper ATK% buffer and Skill DMG Taken debuffer for the team.

**★ Key Notes:**

# Kasimira

If you are using Tilia on another team, Kasimira is the next best choice as she can provide a good amount of Skill DMG Taken debuffs.

**★ Key Notes:**
• Keep the enemy within her Support Skill's radius for consistent Ignis Mark inflicts.
• **Shocking Bombardment** is a consistent source of Skill DMG Taken debuff.
• **Meltdown Threshold** provides a good amount of Skill Crit Rate buff.
• Both **Surprise Gift** and **Lucky Blast** are QoL potentials that can make your rotations better.
