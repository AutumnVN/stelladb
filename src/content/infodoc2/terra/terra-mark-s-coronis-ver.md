# Nazuna

Unlike the original Terra Mark team, Nazuna doesn't have to worry about inflicting Marks all by herself thanks to SCoronis, allowing her to run **Small Force** for better debuffs.

**★ Key Notes:**
• Nazuna has to keep moving so she can regenerate Energy faster.
• **Touch of Nature** is her most important potential. If you don't have it maxed, reset.
• **Small Force** and **Wobble Dance** lets her deployed penguin stack Terra Marks and Terra DMG Taken at the same time.
• **Rocket Cart** provides a lot of value due to its potential in inflicting high amounts of Terra DMG Taken debuff.
• **Swiftstep** is taken if you need the additional dodge and/or extra ATK% buffing.
• **Balloonado** is only considered for challenging Menace Arena.

# Gerie

Gerie is responsible for triggering Nazuna and S. Coronis' Terra Marks, allowing her to do massive damage. Keep in mind that her Support Skill renders her completely stationary which makes her prone to missing hits if you aren't paying attention to your positioning.

**★ Key Notes:**
• **Sin-Bound Echo** allows her to stay longer in the field which enables her to trigger more Marks.
• **Curtain Call** (for single target) and **Deeper into Sin** (for AoE) are interchangeable.
• Mark DMG from her skills scale based on Trekker Tier stage.
• Mark DMG from her emblems interact multiplicatively with **Touch of Nature** and **Terra Chorus**.
• In AoE content, her damage output increases significantly if there are enemies with Terra Mark grouped together due to the quadratic scaling of Terra Mark triggers.

## Skill

1/1/1/1 (Not needed)

Both **Immediate Execution (Support Skill)** and **Divine: Retribution of Evil (Ultimate)** do not need to be upgraded since Mark DMG does not scale from skill levels.

# Springseek Coronis

Springseek Coronis acts as the intended replacement for Tilia in this team. Her role is to provide Mark DMG Taken debuff like Tilia (albeit a bit lower, percentage-wise) plus additional ATK% buffs and Terra DMG Taken debuffs.

**★ Key Notes:**
• **Terra Chorus** is her most important potential. If you don't have this, reset.
• **Lasting Aftertaste** allows her vials to be in-line with Gerie's on-field time.
• Ensure that enemies are within her vials range otherwise, her contribution drops heavily.

## Skill

1/1/1/1 (Not needed)

None of her skills scale buffs or debuffs and thus, it is not worth it to waste resources for her in this team.
