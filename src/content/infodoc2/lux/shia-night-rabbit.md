# Shia

Just as the Snow Rabbit build is recognized as the best boss-fighting composition, the Night Rabbit build is similarly regarded as one of the strongest AoE builds in the game.

**★ Key Notes:**
• This build deals quadratic damage when enemies are clumped up.
• **Wave to Oblivion** is important to have if you plan on using her Ultimate against singular tanky enemies.
• Some Snow Rabbit potentials (**Glacial Dash** and **Subzero Dominion**) are decent to have since her attack pattern still scrolls through the AA1-AA4 sequence.
• **Leporine Gale** is taken in situations where you are able to group up enemies from a distance in order to be able to take advantage of its effects.

## Skill

10/10/1/10 (Auto Attack > Main Skill >= Ultimate)

@skill(Sail of Twin Moons) **Sail of Twin Moons (Auto Attack)** will be the main priority to level for this build.

@skill(Whirling Waves) **Moonlight Overflow** and **Astral Pirouette** scales based on the level of **Whirling Waves (Main Skill)**.

@skill(Divine Deluge) **Divine Deluge (Ultimate)** helps with dealing against tanky enemies. Due to the nature of this build being strictly good against AOE, sudden singular tanky enemies becomes a pain to deal without using Ult.

# Tilia

Tilia is the premier must-have support for Lux teams as she provides Lux DMG Taken debuffs plus ATK% and Mark DMG% buffs along with a consistent Lux Mark application.

**★ Key Notes:**
• The only real difference in this build compared to the Snow Rabbit team is the removal of **Enemy Counter** and **Brilliant Relay**.

## Skill

1/1/10/1 (Support Skill only)

@skill(Aegis Intervention) **Aegis Intervention (Support Skill)** provides an ATK% buff to the team when her skill deals DMG. This buff scales according to the level of the skill and thus, it is beneficial to upgrade it.

# Minova

***Currently in re-evaluation phase**

Minova currently provides the highest and consistent source of ATK% buffs in the game, allowing her to be a great choice in extended boss fights like Boss Blitz.

**★ Key Notes:**
• **Three Base Hit** is specifically useful for this team since it is used against AoE content .
• **Oath Satellite** can replace **Four Star Wanted Level** only if: 1) you need to tank hits and 2) you want to improve the uptime of the Prayer Beneath Light Disc.
• Take **Perfect Arc** if you do not have **Four Star Wanted Level**.

## Skill

1/1/1/1 (Not needed)

Despite Minova's high scaling on her support skill, it is better for her to be relegated as a pure supportive unit for Shia.

# Ann

Ann is a great alternative in case you do not have Minova on your account as she offers plenty of ATK% buffs from her potentials and her Support Skill.

**★ Key Notes:**
• You can constantly have 100% uptime on **Warm Wind Guard**'s entire duration by dashing in and out of it using Shia's dash cancel technique.
• Only take **Leveraged Boost** for fights that contain projectiles.
