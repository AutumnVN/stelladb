# Firenze

Firenze’s Main Skill build focuses on dishing out sustained high skill damage through the synergy of the Nimble Step and High Risk potentials. ★ Key Notes: • Focus on keeping Nimble Step 's domain active to consistently proc the additional damage from High Risk . • Be mindful of Firenze's position when using Main Skill as it is prone to missing its hits due to its inability to be directionally adjusted. • Do not let Firenze be staggered , otherwise, your rotation falls apart in terms of timing. • Place Your Bets is a powerful potential that amplifies her Ultimate's damage output. • Try to wait until Firenze has max stacks of Flash Break before casting her Main Skill or Ultimate.

## Skill

Elegant Parade (Main Skill) will be the main priority to upgrade as not only does it increase its own base multipliers but it also affects Step Kicking and Nimble Step due to their multipliers scaling based on its level. Personal Tuition (Ultimate) is the next priority to upgrade as it offers high damage multipliers by default and does not result in a damage loss, especially if you have Place Your Bets in your Record.

# Cosette

Cosette is the staple support for any Umbra team at the moment due to the consistent high Umbra DMG Taken debuff and ATK% buffs that she is able to provide. ★ Key Notes: • Phantom Mark is preferred over Abyssal Mark as it allows Cosette to be on field longer. • Dark: Mirage is a very good potentia l to take as the 4th potential if used against a single enemy . If used on multiple enemies , it will negatively affect Dark: Afterglow and Dark: Demon Mark 's uptime. • Charge Efficiency from her emblem is only relevant if you have Nether Blaze on your Record.

## Skill

Abyssal Hunter (Support Skill) is the only skill that matters here as it scales the Umbra DMG Taken debuff inflicted based on the level of the skill.

# Otoha - Weeping Sky Build

Otoha's skill build relies on the synergy of Ripple of Shadow , Evening Rai n , and Rain-Woven Sorrow to bombard a specific area with Sweeping Sky. This works best against enemies who are gathered in one place and bosses that are not mobile. ★ Key Notes: • Make sure that enemies are within the bombardment area of Sweeping Sky as this is the only way she can deal damage and inflict debuffs on this build. • Devouring Shadow provides a unique DEF PEN buff that requires Otoha to be in an all-Umbra team to work.

## Skill

Shattered Edge (Support Skill) is the only skill to prioritize for Otoha since both Ripple of Shadow and Evening Rain 's DMG scales based on this skill's level.

# Otoha - Laser Build

Otoha's laser build pales in comparison with her skill build when it comes to providing buffs and debuffs. However, what it brings is the ability to be used against bosses that are mobile . ★ Key Notes: • Solitary Lantern , along with Night-Walker , are the main potentials that enable her to do damage. • Having a +1 level of Solitary Lantern on your emblem is a big priority for this build as its Ultimate Crit Rate buff gets doubled at Lv 7 . • Devouring Shadow provides a unique DEF PEN buff that requires Otoha to be in an all-Umbra team to work. • Ensure that her Support Skill is on cooldown before using her Ultimate to take advantage of the buff from Without A Trace . • Hollow Steps synergizes with Night-Walker and Solitary Lantern as it consistently increase her personal Ultimate Crit Rate and Crit DMG.

## Skill

Shattered Edge (Support Skill) is the only skill to prioritize for Otoha as Night-Walker 's DMG scales based on this skill's level.

# Mistique

If you don't have Otoha, Mistique can take her place as a debuffer with Solar Absorption . She can also transition to being a sub-DPS for the team if heavily invested. ★ Key Notes: • Malice is required to activate the secondary effects of Petals of Blooming Mirage and Phantom of the Banquet 's Melody Skill. • Solar Absorption will be her first and foremost priority for this build since you rely on Firenze to do damage. • The Conjuring must be maxed if you want Mistique's damage contribution to be higher because her damage output heavily relies on how much minions she can summon. • The rest of the potentials are only relevant if you want her to be a sub-DPS . • For emblem investment, only aim for Solar Absorption if you want her to be a pure debuffer .

## Skill

Summoning Circle (Support Skill) is where all of Mistique's DPS comes from. The Minion DMG dealt by her summoned minions scales based on this skill's level.

# Coronis

If you do not have Otoha or Mistique, Coronis is the final alternative to take their place. Her role for the team is to provide ATK% and Umbra DMG Taken debuffs and help with depleting enemy Resilience with her Medical Research potential. ★ Key Notes: • The 5% resilience depletion from Medical Research does not increase further with higher levels.

## Skill

Coronis is only used for her Potential kit and none of her skills scale buffs or debuffs so there is no need to waste resources on her.
