# Canace

Canace is a cheap option to place on the Main slot as she does not need any investments at all to function within the team. Her only purpose is to provide Ventus DMG Taken debuff through her Ventus-imbued Auto Attack bullets along with a slight ATK% buff from **Subject Boost**.

**★ Key Notes:**
• **Most Admired** is important for **Subject Boost** as it increases the ATK% buff that it provides.
• **Erratic Weather** is important for **Weakness Analysis** as it provides more chances of firing Ventus-imbued bullets.
• Both **Comprehensive Praise** and **Shadow Stride** are nice to have but not really needed.

## Skill

1/1/1/1 (Not needed)

Canace is only used for her Potentials and none of her skills provide any buffs for the team or debuffs to the enemies so it is not recommended to level any of them.
