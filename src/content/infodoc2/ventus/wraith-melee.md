# Wraith

Wraith's Auto Attack build utilizes her melee mode to summon tornados with Last Gasp of Gale and Twin Shadows . To maximize the amount of tornados she can summon, do the Main Skill x1 -> Auto Attack -> Main Skill x1 -> Auto Attack rotation. This will allow her to consistently summon and maintain 3 tornados against the enemy, ★ Key Notes: • Unlike other teams, this team thrives on maximizing Melody of Focus notes , since Blade's Waltz , Zero Hour , and Rapid Draw scale poorly with more levels by comparison.

## Skill

Whirling Onslaught (Main Skill) is the main priority to level up for this build since Last Gasp of Gale and Twin Shadows scale with its level. Swish and Fire (Auto Attack) is the second priority, although you can take your time with it as the damage it can contribute is not that much and nothing scales based on its level. Thousand Shade Dance (Ultimate) is the lowest priority since, at most, she can only use it thrice and does not leave that much of an impact compared to the rest.

# Ann

Ann supports the team by giving a lot of Ventus DMG Taken debuffs and ATK% buffs. ★ Key Notes: • Warm Wind Guard requires you to enter directly into the wall and can be unreliable at certain situations. If you find it difficult to do consistently , you can opt to replace it with Gale Bastion . • Both Turbulent Flow and Wind Erosion Havoc requires the enemy to be within the wall .

## Skill

Wind Barrier (Support Skill) provides the team with an ATK% buff that scales based on the skill's level so it is recommended to level this skill.

# Nazuka

If you don't have Eleanor, Nazuka is still able to act as this team’s sub-DPS , contributing around 30% of the total damage (assuming high investment) while doubling as a utility support through Ventus DMG Taken debuffs and ATK% buffs.

## Skill

Bouquet Blast (Support Skill) is where majority of Nazuka's damage output comes from. It is recommended to level it up as much as you can.

# Minova

If you don't have Nazuka, you can use Minova as an alternative. She is able to provide high amounts of ATK% buffs through Star Core Crumbled and Fervent Applause . ★ Key Notes: • Three Base Hit is only able to come online if you are dealing with stages that have at least 3 enemies .

## Skill

Minova is only used for her Potentials and none of her skills provide any buffs for the team or debuffs to the enemies so it is not recommended to level any of them.

# Canace

If you don't have Nazuka or Minova, Canace is the 3rd best option for this team. Her role is to inflict Ventus DMG Taken debuffs using Structure Analysis and Conclusion Analysis as well as provide a subtle amount of ATK% buff to Wraith with Storm Eye Expansion . ★ Key Notes: • Grants Efficient Delving allows Structure Analysis by reducing the attack interval of the laurel wreath. • Grants Speedup Notion synchronizes with Storm Eye Expansion by increasing the rotation speed of the laurel wreath. • The debuff from Conclusion Analysis only works when the enemy's resilience is broken .

## Skill

Canace is only used for her Potentials and none of her skills provide any buffs for the team or debuffs to the enemies so it is not recommended to level any of them.
