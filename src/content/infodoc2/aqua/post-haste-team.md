# Teresa

Teresa takes on the Main slot as a support role of this team as she is able to provide ample amounts of ATK% buffs and Aqua DMG Taken debuffs to enemies that are hit by her Main Skill. ★ Key Notes: • In order • Casting another Main Skill requires at least 0.3s of delay in order to take advantage of Stream: True Charged Strike 's effects. • Stream: Drip's Overturn is mainly used to help Freesia charge her energy faster so she can help out as a secondary DPS earlier. • Both Cordiality and Muddy Engulf will only activate if you hit the enemy. • Magic Sword Siphoning is primarily a QoL potential that helps in making her buff and debuff uptimes be more consistent.

## Skill

Upgrading Limpid Stream (Main Skill) allows Teresa's Surging Shield to absorb damage better. This upgrade is not mandatory . Only do it if you find yourself struggling to survive.

# Freesia

Following the Patch 1.9 adjustments to Ice Vortex , Freesia's contribution now leans towards the Aqua DMG Taken debuff from Winter's Grip and Guardian's Heart 's team-wide Crit Rate buff. ★ Key Notes: • The optional potentials below simply serves as filler potentials so she does not appear as often when you are doing Ascension. • Only Winter's Grip and Guardian's Heart are relevant to her emblem investments if you don't want to cope into building her as a sub-DPS.

## Skill

With the adjustments made to Ice Vortex , it's simply not worth it to invest into her as a sub-DPS for this team for single target boss content . However, if you still want to do so , upgrade Frost Bloom (Support Skill) to max because Localized Snowfall scales based on its level .
