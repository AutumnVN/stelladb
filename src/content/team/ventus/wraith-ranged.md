If you don't have Nazuka, you can use Minova as an alternative. She is able to provide high amounts of ATK% buffs through **Star Core Crumbled** and **Fervent Applause**.

**★ Key Notes:**

# Canace

If you don't have Nazuka or Minova, Canace is the 3rd best option for this team. Her role is to inflict Ventus DMG Taken debuffs using **Structure Analysis** and **Conclusion Analysis** as well as provide a subtle amount of ATK% buff to Wraith with **Storm Eye Expansion**.

**★ Key Notes:**
• **Grants Efficient Delving** allows **Structure Analysis** by reducing the attack interval of the laurel wreath.
• **Grants Speedup Notion** synchronizes with **Storm Eye Expansion** by increasing the rotation speed of the laurel wreath.
• The debuff from **Conclusion Analysis** only works when the enemy's resilience is broken.
