# Nazuna

Nazuna acts as the premier Terra Mark applier and buffer, with **Touch of Nature** providing a massive 420% Mark DMG boost to Terra Trekkers. Keep in mind that she is quite fragile so do not let her take too many hits.

**★ Key Notes:**
• It is important for Nazuna to keep moving so she can regenerate Energy faster.
• **Touch of Nature** is her most important potential. If you don't have it maxed, reset.
• After a hotfix in version 1.7, Nazuna got a shadowfix on **Wobble Dance** that allows her Main Skill's debuff have a 100% uptime, making it better option than **Roll the Dice**.
• **Clean Sweep** is used to apply more Terra Marks on enemies.
• **Rocket Cart** gets a lot of value for its high Terra DMG Taken debuff but its effectiveness varies depending on how many stacks she is able to proc.

# Gerie

Gerie acts as the DPS by triggering Nazuna's Terra Marks. Keep in mind that her Support Skill renders her completely stationary which makes her prone to missing hits if you aren't paying attention to your positioning.

**★ Key Notes:**
• **Sin-Bound Echo** allows her to stay longer in the field which enables her to trigger more Marks.
• **Curtain Call** (for single target) and **Deeper into Sin** (for AoE) are interchangeable.
• Mark DMG from her skills scale based on Trekker Tier stage.
• Mark DMG from her emblems interact multiplicatively with **Touch of Nature** and **Blinding Beam**.
• In AoE content, her damage output increases significantly if there are enemies with Terra Mark grouped together due to the quadratic scaling of Terra Mark triggers.

## Skill

1/1/1/1 (Not needed)

Both **Immediate Execution (Support Skill)** and **Divine: Retribution of Evil (Ultimate)** do not need to be upgraded since Mark DMG does not scale from skill levels.

# Tilia

This team setup is further amplified by Tilia thanks to **Blinding Beam** providing a huge 300% Mark DMG taken debuff at max level whenever she inflicts Lux Mark. Since this is a debuff, it interacts multiplicatively with Nazuna's **Touch of Nature**.

**★ Key Notes:**
• **Blinding Beam** is her most important potential for this team. If you don't have it maxed, reset.
• **Holy Inscription** enables the Mark DMG Taken debuff from **Blinding Beam** to be consistently active as long as the enemy is within the circle it spawns.
• **Renewed Spectrum** is taken to reduce the cooldown of her Support Skill.

## Skill

1/1/10/1 (Support Skill only)

@skill(Aegis Intervention) **Aegis Intervention (Support Skill)** provides an ATK% buff to the team when her skill deals damage. This buff scales according to the level of the skill and thus, it is beneficial to upgrade it.
