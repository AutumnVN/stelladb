# Cosette

Cosette occupies this team's Main slot as she is able to provide high amounts of Umbra DMG Taken debuffs through her potentials and Main Skill.

**★ Key Notes:**
• If you can, use Cosette's Main Skill within the AA1-AA3 attack sequence to prevent **Twilight Wish** stacks from expiring.
• **Nightscar Trace** allows Cosette to use her Main Skill more often because of its synergy with Otoha's ability to dish out Critical hits.
• You can take **Nightshadow Veil** to maintain the Umbra DMG buff gained from Those With Ambition's Harmony Skill or to simply have better survivability.

# Otoha

Otoha's laser build revolves on the Soul Rend effect applied by Weeping Sky. This is a mandatory condition for both **Night-Walker**'s Doomfall procs and the 80% triple-laser chance from **Solitary Lantern**. In contrast to other team comps, this build deals Ultimate DMG (not to be confused with a traditional Ultimate build like Fuyuka).

**★ Key Notes:**
• **Devouring Shadow** provides a unique DEF PEN buff that requires Otoha to be in an all-Umbra team to work.
• Do not let her Support Skill miss as this is the only way to inflict Soul Rend to the enemy.
• Try to use her Ultimate while her Support Skill is on cooldown to take advantage of **Without A Trace**.
• **Hollow Steps** allows Otoha to frequently do critical hits, synergizing with Cosette's **Nightscar Trace** potential.
• Having a +1 level of **Solitary Lantern** on your emblem is a big priority for this build as its Ultimate Crit Rate buff gets doubled at Lv 7.

# Coronis

If you do not have Mistique, Coronis is the final alternative to take her place. Her role for the team is to provide ATK% and Umbra DMG Taken debuffs and help with depleting enemy Resilience with her **Medical Research** potential.

**★ Key Notes:**
• The 5% resilience depletion from **Medical Research** does not increase further with higher levels.

1/1/1/1 (Not needed)
