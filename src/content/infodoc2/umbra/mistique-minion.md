# Cosette

Among the current options for Umbra, Cosette is the best Trekker to placed in the Main Slot for this team. Her only purpose is to provide Umbra-related buffs for Mistique's minions. Due to how peculiar minions are on buff interactions, there are barely any potentials that can reliably be used for this team. ★ Key Notes: • Only use Cosette's Main Skill within the AA1-AA3 attack sequence to prevent Twilight Wish stacks from expiring. • Netherworld Touch enables the Umbra DMG taken debuff from Nocturn Mark to have a permanent uptime. • You can take Nightshadow Veil to maintain the Umbra DMG buff gained from Those With Ambition 's Harmony Skill or to simply have better survivability .

## Skill

Nocturn Hooks (Main Skill) is the only skill that you should upgrade since it scales the amount of Umbra DMG Taken debuff based on its level.

# Mistique

Contrary to other team compositions, Mistique's DPS capability shines when placed in the Support slot . With the combination of The Conjuring and Twin Born potentials, she is able to spawn a sheer amount of minions that deal high amounts of AoE damage. ★ Key Notes: • Make sure to group up enemies in order to take full advantage of her minions' small range explosions. • Try to keep enemies within Mistique's circle to benefit from Solar Absorption 's debuff.

## Skill

Summoning Circle (Support Skill) is where all of Mistique's DPS comes from. The Minion DMG dealt by her summoned minions scales based on this skill's level.

# Iris

Iris provides a powerful crowd control effect and high ATK% buffs that Mistique's minions can freely benefit from. Her wide-range Support Skill can pull and lock enemies in place while buffing allied units within its zone. If you have Frozen Shield , her Ultimate will also buff allied units within its vicinity. ★ Key Notes: • Sneaky Fisher and Prolonged Winter are all you need to make her work for this team composition. • Despite the fact that Frozen Shield says squadmate on its tooltip, it has been tested and proven that it does work for Mistique's minions. • Only take Charge Efficiency on her emblems if you have Frozen Shield in your Record.

## Skill

Iris is only used for her crowd control capability and buffs from potentials . None of her skills scale buffs or debuffs so there is no need to waste resources on her.

# Coronis

Coronis is potent against boss fights due to her ability to provide constant Resilience shred with Medical Research . ★ Key Notes: • Medical Research is the primary reason why Coronis is taken for the team. If your Record does not have it, don't expect her to do much. • The 5% resilience depletion from Medical Research does not increase further with higher levels.

## Skill

Coronis is only used for her Potential kit and none of her skills scale buffs or debuffs so there is no need to waste resources on her.

# Chixia

Chixia provides a higher ATK% buff ceiling but lesser uptime than Iris, with the additional benefit of providing Crit Rate for Mistique's minions. Keep in mind that majority of her buffs require build-up for maximum effect as she has to summon her dragons gradually. ★ Key Notes: • Crimson Dragon: Convergence enables Horn Call and Barrier Construct to provide more buffs. • Crimson Dragon: Chant allows the uptime of Ambush to be more consistent. • Make sure that Mistique's minions explode within the circle of the Crimson Dragons so they can benefit from the Crit Rate buff.

## Skill

Chixia is used for her valuable Potential kit as none of her usable skills scale buffs or debuffs so there is no need to waste skill upgrades on her.
