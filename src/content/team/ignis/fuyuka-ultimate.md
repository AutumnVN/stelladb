# Fuyuka

Fuyuka's Ultimate-focused build is fully reliant on having fast Energy charging along with maxing out **Peak Performance** and **Ironfist Blow**. Keep in mind that this build is primarily used in endgame single target content like Boss Blitz and Finale Echoing.

**★ Key Notes:**
• Currently, any rarity of Charge Efficiency is fine on one of her emblems as she is able to pull off four Ultimates even at the lowest rarity.

## Skill

1/1+/1/10 (Ultimate > Main Skill)

@skill(Blaze Unsealed) **Blaze Unsealed (Ultimate)** is the main priority to upgrade as it encompasses majority of her damage output thanks to its inherent multipliers that are further strengthened by **Peak Performance** and **Ironfist Blow**.

@skill(Ignis Infusion) **Ignis Infusion (Main Skill)** is the last priority as it is only used to comfortably bring down the Resilience bar of the boss for her first Ultimate setup. Additionally, **Kitty Punch** and **Multi-Shot** also scales with its level.

# Flora

Flora's primary role is to provide consistent Ignis DMG taken debuffs and Crit-related buffs through her Soulflare and Mindflare Butterflies. Although you have to be mindful on the timing of her Support Skill's usage.

**★ Key Notes:**
• **Ember of Tomorrow** amplifies the Ignis DMG Taken debuff from her Soulflare Butterflies.
• **Ashes of the Past** is preferable to **Ember Rekindled** for the additional Crit Rate and bonus Crit DMG that it provides.
• **Reinforced Impression** allows for more Crit Rate gain which helps in lessening the crit mald.
• The debuff applied by **Lethal Inferno** stacks independently from the Soulflare Butterflies of her Support Skill rather than refreshing the same duration.

## Skill

1/1/10/1 (Support Skill only)

@skill(Flutter Flare) Only upgrade **Flutter Flare (Support Skill)** as it increases the Crit DMG given by Mindflare Butterfly. Keep in mind that the Crit DMG buff does not correlate with the amount of Mindflare Butterflies present.

# Snowish Laru

Snowish Laru is currently a better pick than Chixia as she is able to provide instantaneous high amounts of ATK% buffs and Ignis DMG Taken debuff from her potentials.

**★ Key Notes:**
• **Fairy Tale Rule** provides a huge amount of Ignis DMG Taken debuff but only if the team is composed of Ignis Trekkers.
• **Two-Gun Salute** is only taken if you have Snowy Night Surprise disc for rotation optimization purposes.
• **Heart's Benediction** is only taken if you have Snowy Night Surprise disc. Do not consider taking it otherwise.

**For Finale Echoing:**
• If your investments are quite high, **Toy Army** is not that much of a priority to max out compared to Boss Blitz.

## Skill

1/1/1/1 (Not needed)

For this team, Snowish Laru is used for her valuable Potential kit as none of her usable skills scale buffs or debuffs so there is no need to waste skill upgrades on her.

# Chixia

Chixia provides lots of ATK% buffs for the team through her potentials, although unlike Snowish Laru, she needs some time to build-up the buffs from certain potentials.

**★ Key Notes:**
• **Ink Sigil: Horn Call**'s ATK% buff scales based on the amount of Chixia's minions in the field.
• **Prayer of Raging Flame** relies on hitting multiple targets to stack up the ATK% buff it provides. It will never stack if it is only hitting one target.

## Skill

1/1/1/1 (Not needed)

For this team, Chixia is used for her valuable Potential kit as none of her usable skills scale buffs or debuffs so there is no need to waste skill upgrades on her.

# Rotation

The rotation timing varies slightly depending on how many Toy Army buff stacks you want Fuyuka's first Ultimate hit to land on.

### General Rotation
__Flora (with Reincarnation of Fire)__
1. XLaru Supp. Skill + Ultimate
2. Flora Ultimate
3. Flora Supp. Skill
4. Wait 2s then use Fuyuka's Ultimate

__Flora (without Reincarnation of Fire)__
1. Flora Ultimate
2. Wait 2s and then use XLaru Supp. Skill + Ultimate
3. Wait 3s and then use Flora Supp. Skill
4. Wait 2s then use Fuyuka's Ultimate
