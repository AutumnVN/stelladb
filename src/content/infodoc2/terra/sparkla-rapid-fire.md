# Tilia

If you do not have Springseek Coronis, Tilia can take her place as a hyper ATK% buffer and Skill DMG Taken debuffer for the team. ★ Key Notes: • Perfect Spiral provides a decent amount Skill DMG Taken debuff. • The rest of her potentials are what makes up the huge ATK% buffs that she can provide to the team.

## Skill

Aegis Intervention (Support Skill) provides an ATK% buff to the team when her skill deals damage. This buff scales according to the level of the skill and thus, it is beneficial to upgrade it.
