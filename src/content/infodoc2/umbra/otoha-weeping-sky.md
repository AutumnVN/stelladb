# Cosette

Cosette occupies this team's Main slot as she is able to provide high amounts of Umbra DMG Taken debuffs through her potentials and Main Skill. ★ Key Notes: • If you can, use Cosette's Main Skill within the AA1-AA3 attack sequence to prevent Twilight Wish stacks from expiring. • You can take Nightshadow Veil to maintain the Umbra DMG buff gained from Those With Ambition 's Harmony Skill or to simply have better survivability .

## Skill

Nocturn Hooks (Main Skill) is the only skill that you should upgrade since it scales the amount of Umbra DMG Taken debuff based on its level.

# Otoha

Otoha's skill build relies on the synergy of Ripple of Shadow , Evening Rai n , and Rain-Woven Sorrow to bombard a specific area with Sweeping Sky. This works best against enemies who are gathered in one place and bosses that are not mobile. ★ Key Notes: • Make sure that enemies are within the bombardment area of Sweeping Sky as this is the only way she can deal damage on this build. • Devouring Shadow provides a unique DEF PEN buff that requires Otoha to be in an all-Umbra team to work.

## Skill

Shattered Edge (Support Skill) is the only skill to prioritize for Otoha since both Ripple of Shadow and Evening Rain 's DMG scales based on this skill's level.

# Mistique

Mistique acts as a sub-DPS and debuffer for the team using her typical minion build plus Solar Absorption . ★ Key Notes: • If you need crowd control more than her damage output, replace Happy Face and Reality Erosion with Slumber Invitation and Lucky Constitution .

## Skill

Summoning Circle (Support Skill) is where all of Mistique's DPS comes from. The Minion DMG dealt by her summoned minions scales based on this skill's level.

# Coronis

If you do not have Mistique, Coronis is the final alternative to take her place. Her role for the team is to provide ATK% and Umbra DMG Taken debuffs and help with depleting enemy Resilience with her Medical Research potential. ★ Key Notes: • The 5% resilience depletion from Medical Research does not increase further with higher levels.

## Skill

Coronis is only used for her Potential kit and none of her skills scale buffs or debuffs so there is no need to waste resources on her.
