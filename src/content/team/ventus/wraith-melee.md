# Wraith

Wraith's Auto Attack build utilizes her melee mode to summon tornados with **Last Gasp of Gale** and **Twin Shadows**. To maximize the amount of tornados she can summon, do the Main Skill x1 -> Auto Attack -> Main Skill x1 -> Auto Attack rotation. This will allow her to consistently summon and maintain 3 tornados against the enemy,

**★ Key Notes:**
• Unlike other teams, this team thrives on maximizing Melody of Focus notes, since **Blade's Waltz**, **Zero Hour**, and **Rapid Draw** scale poorly with more levels by comparison.

## Skill

1+/10/1/1+ (Main Skill >>> Auto Attack > Ultimate)

@skill(Whirling Onslaught) **Whirling Onslaught (Main Skill)** is the main priority to level up for this build since **Last Gasp of Gale** and **Twin Shadows** scale with its level.

@skill(Swish and Fire) **Swish and Fire (Auto Attack)** is the second priority, although you can take your time with it as the damage it can contribute is not that much and nothing scales based on its level.

@skill(Thousand Shade Dance) **Thousand Shade Dance (Ultimate)** is the lowest priority since, at most, she can only use it thrice and does not leave that much of an impact compared to the rest.

# Ann

Ann supports the team by giving a lot of Ventus DMG Taken debuffs and ATK% buffs.

**★ Key Notes:**
• **Warm Wind Guard** requires you to enter directly into the wall and can be unreliable at certain situations. If you find it difficult to do consistently, you can opt to replace it with **Gale Bastion**.
• Both **Turbulent Flow** and **Wind Erosion Havoc** requires the enemy to be within the wall.

## Skill

1/1/10/1 (Support Skill only)

@skill(Wind Barrier) **Wind Barrier (Support Skill)** provides the team with an ATK% buff that scales based on the skill's level so it is recommended to level this skill.

# Canace

If you don't have Nazuka or Minova, Canace is the 3rd best option for this team. Her role is to inflict Ventus DMG Taken debuffs using **Structure Analysis** and **Conclusion Analysis** as well as provide a subtle amount of ATK% buff to Wraith with **Storm Eye Expansion**.

**★ Key Notes:**
• **Grants Efficient Delving** allows **Structure Analysis** by reducing the attack interval of the laurel wreath.
• **Grants Speedup Notion** synchronizes with **Storm Eye Expansion** by increasing the rotation speed of the laurel wreath.
• The debuff from **Conclusion Analysis** only works when the enemy's resilience is broken.

# Rotation

Wraith comps are generally auto-friendly that can have optimizations to squeeze more damage.

### General Rotation
1. Use both Support Skills
2. Use Wraith skills while weaving a few auto attacks between each skill activation to proc her tornadoes

### When to use Ultimate?
• Wraith: Whenever there's a need to dodge big attacks from enemies (preferably also while waiting for her supports' skills cooldown to come back up).
• Nazuka: Preferably when there's a break window and also when her Support Skill CD is around 7s+ to not delay its cast.
• Ann: To block target attacks or disrupt disruptable enemies.

### Tips
• Wraith: Use her Main Skill sparingly (keeping it at 2 stacks always) while waiting for Ann's Support Skill to snapshot her tornadoes with all of Ann's buffs.
