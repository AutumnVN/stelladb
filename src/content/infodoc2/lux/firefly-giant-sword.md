# Minova

Minova's role in this team is to provide Lux DMG Taken debuffs with her **More Than Just Pretty** potential.

**★ Key Notes:**
• Space out Minova's Main Skill usage by only using each phase after every AA5 hit so **More Than Just Pretty**'s uptime is constantly active.
• Minova's AA5 hit has a distinctive \*ding\* sound cue for you to easily determine when to use her Main Skill again.
• Both **Ashwind Rhythm** and **Light Burn** are treated as QoL potentials in this case because they are only taken for their unique effects and not for the Skill DMG buff that they provide.

## Skill

1/1/1/1 (Not needed)

Minova is relegated as a supporter for Firefly on this team so it's best to not upgrade any of her skills.

# Firefly

**Currently in testing phase.**

Firefly's Giant Sword build relies heavily on Auto Attack DMG . This is currently her best solo DPS build because none of the current units have Lucent mark trigger (except Firefly herself on Main slot).

**★ Key Notes:**
• **Radiance Blade: Blazing Edge** requires a full Lux team to function.
• **Luminous Onslaught** requires consistent Mark triggers to stack its effects, which Minova cannot do so it is not included in this build.

## Skill

1/1/10/1 (Support Skill only)

@skill(Starflare Pursuit) **Starflare Pursuit (Support Skill)** is the only important skill to max out as this is Firefly's prominent source of damage. Additionally, both **Radiance Blade: Blazing Edge** and **Radiance Blade: Pursuing Slash**'s DMG output scale with its level.

# Tilia

Tilia is a staple support for any Lux team currently, offering constant ATK% buffs and Lux DMG Taken debuffs for the team using her Support Skill and potentials.

**★ Key Notes:**
• **Blinding Beam** gets a place as an optional potential for this team because Minova's raw Mark DMG is high (in fact, the highest in the game currently). If you are forced to take a 5th Tilia potential, it's pretty much the last best choice .

## Skill

1/1/10/1 (Support Skill only)

@skill(Aegis Intervention) **Aegis Intervention (Support Skill)** provides an ATK% buff to the team when her skill deals DMG. This buff scales according to the level of the skill and thus, it is beneficial to upgrade it.

# Rotation

This team generally relies on Minova and Tilia to support Firefly to do as much damage as possible with their potentials.

### General Rotation
1. Firefly Supp. Skill
2. Tilia Supp. Skill
3. Minova full Auto Attack chain (she has 5 AA hits)
4. Minova Main Skill

### When to use Ultimate?
• Firefly: When there is around 5s left in her Support Skill's cooldown.
• Tilia: Whenever available as long as her skill is not cancelled and Firefly is in the battlefield.

### Tips
• Firefly takes 2.4s to start attacking so it is fine to deploy her early on.
• Using Minova's skill after every 5th AA hit allows for **More Than Just Pretty** to have constant uptime.
• Her 5th AA hit has a unique \*ding\* sound cue to aid you on when to use her Main Skill again.
