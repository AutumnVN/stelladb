# Firenze

Firenze's Minion build is primarily relevant in Menace Arena usage because of the far reaching range of her minions. Since this is a minion build, it means that only a few ATK buffs will affect her summoned clones.

**★ Key Notes:**
• **Shadow Step** incentivizes mono-Umbra builds by increasing her maximum clone count for every Umbra teammate in the party.
• **Phantom Waltz**, and **Gloomy Veil** further increases the maximum amount of clones that she can spawn.
• Having **Place Your Bets** and **Instant Liquidation** in your Record allows her Ultimate to become stronger.
• To maximize **Instant Liquidation**'s effect, there must be 5 shadow clones in the field.

## Skill

10/10/1/10 (Auto Attack > Main Skill > Ultimate)

@skill(Blooming Shadows) **Blooming Shadows (Auto Attack)** is the primary priority to upgrade as her clones' damage directly scale based on its level.

@skill(Elegant Parade) **Elegant Parade (Main Skill)** is the second priority to upgrade since **Shadow Step**'s damage scales based on its level.

@skill(Personal Tuition) Lastly, **Personal Tuition (Ultimate)** will be the final priority for its ability to deal burst damage every once in a while, which gets better if you have **Place Your Bets** and **Instant Liquidation** in your Record.

# Cosette

Cosette is the staple support for this team primarily for the consistent high Umbra DMG Taken debuff and ATK% buffs that she is able to provide for the team.

**★ Key Notes:**
• **Phantom Mark** is preferred over **Abyssal Mark** as it allows her to be on field longer.
• Charge Efficiency from her emblem is only relevant if you have **Nether Blaze** on your Record.

1/1/10/1 (Support Skill only)

@skill(Abyssal Hunter) **Abyssal Hunter (Support Skill)** is the only skill that matters here as it scales the Umbra DMG Taken debuff inflicted based on the level of the skill.

# Otoha

Otoha acts as a direct replacement for Mistique for this team. Her role is to be a sub-DPS with the capability to buff the team with **Devouring Shadow**.

**★ Key Notes:**
• **Solitary Lantern**, along with **Night-Walker**, are what allows her to become a suitable sub-DPS for this team.
• **Devouring Shadow** is unique and powerful as it is currently the only source of DEF PEN in the game. Keep in mind that it requires Otoha to be in an all-Umbra team for it to work.
• Ensure that her Support Skill is on cooldown before using her Ultimate to take advantage of the buff from **Without A Trace**.
• **Hollow Steps** synergizes with **Night-Walker** and **Solitary Lantern** as it consistently increase her personal Ultimate Crit Rate and Crit DMG.

## Skill

1/1/10/1 (Support Skill only)

@skill(Shattered Edge) **Shattered Edge (Support Skill)** is the only skill to prioritize for Otoha as **Night-Walker**'s DMG scales based on this skill's level.

# Mistique

Mistique's role for this team is to provide a powerful crowd control with **Lucky Constitution** and some Umbra DMG Taken debuffs from **Solar Absorption**.

**★ Key Notes:**
• You may consider taking **Extra Perk** if Firenze is struggling to survive.

# Coronis

If you do not have Otoha or Mistique, Coronis is the final alternative to take their place. Her role for the team is to provide ATK% and Umbra DMG Taken debuffs and help with depleting enemy Resilience with her **Medical Research** potential.

**★ Key Notes:**
• The 5% resilience depletion from **Medical Research** does not increase further with higher levels.
