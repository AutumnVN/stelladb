# Shia

Just as the Snow Rabbit build is recognized as the best boss-fighting composition, the Night Rabbit build is similarly regarded as one of the strongest AoE builds in the game. ★ Key Notes: • This build deals quadratic damage when enemies are clumped up. • Wave to Oblivion is important to have if you plan on using her Ultimate against singular tanky enemies. • Some Snow Rabbit potentials ( Glacial Dash and Subzero Dominion ) are decent to have since her attack pattern still scrolls through the AA1-AA4 sequence. • Leporine Gale is taken in situations where you are able to group up enemies from a distance in order to be able to take advantage of its effects.

## Skill

Sail of Twin Moons (Auto Attack) will be the main priority to level for this build. Moonlight Overflow scales based on its skill level. Astral Pirouette scales based on the level of this skill. Divine Deluge (Ultimate) helps with dealing against tanky enemies. Due to the nature of this build being strictly good against AOE, sudden singular tanky enemies becomes a pain to deal without using Ult.

# Tilia

Tilia is the premier must-have support for Lux teams as she provides Lux DMG Taken debuffs plus ATK% and Mark DMG% buffs along with a consistent Lux Mark application. ★ Key Notes: • The only real difference in this build compared to the Snow Rabbit team is the removal of Enemy Counter and Brilliant Relay .

## Skill

Aegis Intervention (Support Skill) provides an ATK% buff to the team when her skill deals DMG. This buff scales according to the level of the skill and thus, it is beneficial to upgrade it.

# Minova

*Currently in re-evaluation phase Minova currently provides the highest and consistent source of ATK% buffs in the game, allowing her to be a great choice in extended boss fights like Boss Blitz. ★ Key Notes: • Three Base Hit is specifically useful for this team since it is used against AoE content . • Oath Satellite can replace Four Star Wanted Level only if : 1) you need to tank hits and 2) you want to improve the uptime of the Prayer Beneath Light Disc. • Take Perfect Arc if you do not have Four Star Wanted Level .
