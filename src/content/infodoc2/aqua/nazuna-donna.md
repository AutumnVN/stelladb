# Nazuna

Nazuna takes on the Main slot as a buffer for this team, providing high amounts of ATK% buff with **Fill the Gap** + **Cutie's Rally** and Skill Crit Rate with **Operation: Pandemonium**.

**★ Key Notes:**
• **Swiftstep** is only taken if you need the extra dodge count.

## Skill

1/1/1/1 (Not needed)

None of Nazuna's skills benefit this team as they are all Terra-aligned so don't bother leveling any of them for this team.

# Freesia

Following the Patch 1.9 adjustments to **Ice Vortex**, Freesia's contribution now leans towards the Aqua DMG Taken debuff from **Winter's Grip** and **Guardian's Heart**'s team-wide Crit Rate buff.

**★ Key Notes:**
• The optional potentials below simply serves as filler potentials so she does not appear as often when you are doing Ascension.
• Only **Winter's Grip** and **Guardian's Heart** are relevant to her emblem investments if you don't want to cope into building her as a sub-DPS.

## Skill

1/1/1/1 (Not needed)

With the adjustments made to **Ice Vortex**, it's simply not worth it to invest into her as a sub-DPS for this team for single target boss content.

However, if you still want to do so, upgrade **Frost Bloom (Support Skill)** to max because **Localized Snowfall** scales based on its level.
