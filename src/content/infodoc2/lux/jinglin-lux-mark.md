# Jinglin

For a 4-star Trekker, Jinglin has an innately high Mark DMG multiplier. This is further amplified primarily through her own potentials as well as Tilia's **Blinding Beam**.

**★ Key Notes:**
• This team is primarily used in AoE content like Menace Arena.
• if you want to use it against single target bosses, Jinglin has to dash after every skill cast to prevent wasting all 4 stacks in one go because Mark triggers have a 1s internal cooldown.
• **Conductor Conversion** is the most important potential to take in order to amplify her Mark DMG output.
• **Leakage Protection** allows her Ultimate to also trigger Marks, with its scaling being the same as her Main Skill's Mark DMG.
• **Lord of Beasts** is only taken for its ATK interval reduction effect to stack **Auspicious Spirit** faster.

## Skill

As none of her skills affect her Mark DMG, it is not recommended to level up any of them.

# Tilia

Tilia's primary role is to further improve Jinglin's ability to dish out higher Mark DMG through her **Blinding Beam** potential.

**★ Key Notes:**
• If your Record does not have **Blinding Beam**, restart.
• **Enemy Counter** is only relevant against boss stages.

## Skill

1/1/10/1 (Support Skill only)

@skill(Aegis Intervention) **Aegis Intervention (Support Skill)** provides an ATK% buff to the team when her skill deals DMG. This buff scales according to the level of the skill and thus, it is beneficial to upgrade it.

# Firefly

**Currently in testing phase.**

Firefly's Giant Sword build relies heavily on Auto Attack DMG. This is currently her best DPS build because none of the current units have Lucent mark trigger (except Firefly herself on Main slot).

**★ Key Notes:**
• **Radiance Blade: Blazing Edge** requires a full Lux team to function.

## Skill

1/1/10/1 (Support Skill only)

@skill(Starflare Pursuit) **Starflare Pursuit (Support Skill)** is the only important skill to max out as this is Firefly's prominent source of damage. Additionally, both **Radiance Blade: Blazing Edge** and **Radiance Blade: Pursuing Slash**'s DMG output scale with its level.

# Minova

Minova currently provides the highest and consistent source of ATK% buffs in the game.

**★ Key Notes:**
• **Three Base Hit** is specifically useful for this team since it is used against AoE content.
• **Radiant Burst** is taken for its Attack Range buff for Astral Hex.
