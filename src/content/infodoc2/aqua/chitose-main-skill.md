# Chitose

Some of Chitose's Skill-based potentials have been buffed thoroughly in Patch 1.9 and finally caught up with her AA build. However, this team composition is not auto-friendly if you do not have Suntide Willow.

**★ Key Notes:**
• The combination of **Against the Flow** and **Returning Tide** allows Chitose to generate a lot of energy for the team.

## Skill

@skill(Torrent Flash) **Torrent Flash (Main Skill)** is her main damage source for this build as all of her potentials buff up her Skill DMG. **Cascade of Ruin**'s damage multiplier also scales with its level.

# Suntide Willow

Suntide Willow is a direct upgrade to Teresa as the core support of the team. The obvious upgrade is the significantly larger Support Skill radius for your Main Trekker to move around. Additionally, her skill can easily maintain a permanent uptime which keeps her buffs (and **Azure Embrace** from Chitose) active at all times.

On a different note, she is usable outside of Aqua as some of her buffing Potentials do not have an element restriction.

**★ Key Notes:**
• **Rhythm Surge** and **Prismatic Bubbles** provides a total of 30% Crit Rate buff for the team.
• **Engulfing Tide** expands her skill's radius further while giving Aqua DMG buff for the team.
• **Marine Justice** inflicts a good amount of Aqua DMG Taken debuff.
• **Receding Echoes** provides Crit DMG buff for the team.
• **Balmy Surf** provides DEF PEN buff based on the number of Aqua Trekkers in the team.

## Skill

1/1/1+/1+ (Support Skill > Ultimate)

@skill(Frolic in the Sea) @skill(Beach Warning) Prioritize upgrading **Frolic in the Sea (Support Skill)** first and **Beach Warning (Ultimate)** next since her Support Skill gives more buffs than her Ultimate.

Unfortunately, the buff scaling for both skills is a bit underwhelming compared to other Trekkers. Additionally, none of her Potentials for this team scale based on Support Skill level. Therefore, you can comfortably stop at Level 7 if you're low on resources.

# Donna

Donna is the sub-DPS of the team, able to dish out good amounts of damage and buffs for the team.

**★ Key Notes:**
• At least one level of **Chilling Onslaught** is required in order for **Aquatic Assault** to work.
• If you want Donna to just fully support Chitose, you can replace **Chilling Onslaught** and **Aquatic Assault** with **Messenger Arrow**.

## Skill

1/1/1+/1+ (Support Skill > Ultimate)

@skill(Tide Threader) **Tide Threader (Support Skill)** is Donna's primary damage source through its innately high multipliers. Its level also directly scales the damage dealt by **Unleashed Arrow** and **Arrowfall Reckoning**.

# Teresa

Teresa provides the team with good amounts of ATK buffs through her potentials and a huge Aqua DMG Taken debuff if you have **Wave Breaker**.

**★ Key Notes:**
• **Hold On!** is taken for its skill reduction time whenever Chitose gets hit.
• Charge Efficiency on her emblems allows her to cast Ultimate 3-4 times, which is only relevant if you have **Wave Breaker**.

## Skill

1/1/1+/1 (Support Skill only, otherwise not needed)

@skill(Billowing Tide) Upgrading **Billowing Tide (Support Skill)** allows Teresa's Surging Shield to absorb damage better.
This upgrade is not mandatory. Only do it if you find yourself getting hit often.

# Iris

Iris is the final alternative for this team if you are missing a third slotter.

**★ Key Notes:**
• Prioritize upgrading **Bundle Sale** and **Sneaky Fisher** over **Forced Liquidation**.
• Both **Raging Blizzard** and **Prolonged Winter** are only used for their unique effect.
