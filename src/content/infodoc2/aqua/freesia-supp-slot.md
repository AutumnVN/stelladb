# Teresa

Teresa is the core support of the team as she is able to provide high Auto ATK and ATK% buffs through her potentials. ★ Key Notes: • Isn't it Awesome could stay at Lv. 1 for its 25% Attack Speed bonus until Shortcut Seeker is maxed out. • Isn't it Awesome is a high-risk, high-reward potential that needs you to maintain her shield throughout its entire duration (meaning avoiding taking any damage that can deplete the shield). • Hold On! is a nice optional potential to pick up if you have a surplus in potentials.

## Skill

Upgrading Billowing Tide (Support Skill) allows Teresa's Surging Shield to absorb damage better. This upgrade is not mandatory . Only do it if you find yourself getting hit often.

# Freesia

*As of Patch 1.9, Ice Vortex has been changed to only stack one time per enemy instead of three. So this build is not as good as it was pre-patch. Freesia's DPS build revolves around the Localized Snowfall potential. This potential enables her Auto Attacks to have a 30% chance to spawn Ice Flowers within 6s after using her Support Skill in Berserk state. ★ Key Notes: • Ice Vortex provides a significant amount of Crit DMG buff that scales dynamically based on the number of enemies hit on the field. • Penetrating Chill and Winter's Grip stacks up faster when she is near the enemy . • Blessing of Faith is only taken for its 3s Berserk extension effect. • Power of Redemption is only taken if you have a surplus for potentials since she is not able to fully utilize it.

## Skill

Freesia's damage output is fully reliant on Frost Bloom (Support Skill) since this is where the hidden scaling of Localized Snowfall depends on so make sure to upgrade it as much as you can.

# Tilia

Tilia acts as the baseline buffer for this team as it is easier to make a Record with her since she only needs two potentials in order to provide high ATK% buffs.

## Skill

Aegis Intervention (Support Skill) provides an ATK% buff to the team when her skill deals DMG. This buff scales according to the level of the skill and thus, it is beneficial to upgrade it.

# Iris

Iris is a better option as the 2nd Support slot, providing a higher ATK% buff plus an Aqua DMG Taken debuff from her potentials. ★ Key Notes: • Blizzard: Stack and Blizzard: Impact are taken in order for Bundle Sale to hit more reliably against bosses that spawn additional enemies .

## Skill

Iris is only used for her valuable Potential kit and none of her skills scale buffs or debuffs so there is no need to waste resources on her.
