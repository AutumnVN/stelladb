# Nazuna

Unlike the original Terra Mark team, Nazuna doesn’t have to worry about inflicting Marks all by herself thanks to SCoronis, allowing her to run Small Force for better debuffs. ★ Key Notes: • Nazuna has to keep moving so she can regenerate Energy faster . • Touch of Nature is her most important potential . If you don't have it maxed, reset. • Small Force and Wobble Dance lets her deployed penguin stack Terra Marks and Terra DMG Taken at the same time. • Rocket Cart provides a lot of value due to its potential in inflicting high amounts of Terra DMG Taken debuff. • Swiftstep is taken if you need the additional dodge and/or extra ATK% buffing . • Balloonado is only considered for challenging Menace Arena.

## Skill

Plushie Dance (Main Skill) is the primary priority as it provides Terra DMG Taken debuff to enemies, which scales based on the skill's level. Dream Spiral (Ultimate) is the last priority as it provides the team an ATK% buff that scales based on the skill's level.

# Gerie

Gerie is responsible for triggering Nazuna and S. Coronis' Terra Marks , allowing her to do massive damage. Keep in mind that her Support Skill renders her completely stationary which makes her prone to missing hits if you aren't paying attention to your positioning. ★ Key Notes: • Sin-Bound Echo allows her to stay longer in the field which enables her to trigger more Marks. • Curtain Call (for single target) and Deeper into Sin (for AoE) are interchangeable . • Mark DMG from her skills scale based on Trekker Tier stage . • Mark DMG from her emblems interact multiplicatively with Touch of Nature and Terra Chorus . • In AoE content, her damage output increases significantly if there are enemies with Terra Mark grouped together due to the quadratic scaling of Terra Mark triggers.

## Skill

Both Immediate Execution (Support Skill) and Divine: Retribution of Evil (Ultimate) do not need to be upgraded since Mark DMG does not scale from skill levels .

# Springseek Coronis

Springseek Coronis acts as the intended replacement for Tilia in this team. Her role is to provide Mark DMG Taken debuff like Tilia (albeit a bit lower, percentage-wise) plus additional ATK% buffs and Terra DMG Taken debuffs. ★ Key Notes: • Terra Chorus is her most important potential. If you don't have this, reset . • Lasting Aftertaste allows her vials to be in-line with Gerie's on-field time. • Ensure that enemies are within her vials range otherwise, her contribution drops heavily.

## Skill

None of her skills scale buffs or debuffs and thus, it is not worth it to waste resources for her in this team.

# Nazuna

Nazuna acts as the premier Terra Mark applier and buffer , with Touch of Nature providing a massive 420% Mark DMG boost to Terra Trekkers. Keep in mind that she is quite fragile so do not let her take too many hits. ★ Key Notes: • It is important for Nazuna to keep moving so she can regenerate Energy faster . • Touch of Nature is her most important potential. If you don't have it maxed, reset . • After a hotfix in version 1.7, Nazuna got a shadowfix on Wobble Dance that allows her Main Skill's debuff have a 100% uptime , making it better option than Roll the Dice . • Clean Sweep is used to apply more Terra Marks on enemies. • Rocket Cart gets a lot of value for its high Terra DMG Taken debuff but its effectiveness varies depending on how many stacks she is able to proc.

## Skill

Plushie Dance (Main Skill) is the primary priority as it provides Terra DMG Taken debuff to enemies, which scales based on the skill's level. Dream Spiral (Ultimate) is the last priority as it provides the team an ATK% buff that scales based on the skill's level.

# Tilia

This team setup is further amplified by Tilia thanks to Blinding Beam providing a huge 300% Mark DMG taken debuff at max level whenever she inflicts Lux Mark . Since this is a debuff, it interacts multiplicatively with Nazuna's Touch of Nature . ★ Key Notes: • Blinding Beam is her most important potential for this team. If you don't have it maxed, reset . • Holy Inscription enables the Mark DMG Taken debuff from Blinding Beam to be consistently active as long as the enemy is within the circle it spawns. • Renewed Spectrum is taken to reduce the cooldown of her Support Skill.

## Skill

Aegis Intervention (Support Skill) provides an ATK% buff to the team when her skill deals damage. This buff scales according to the level of the skill and thus, it is beneficial to upgrade it.
