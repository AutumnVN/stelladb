# Mistique

The Mistique (Main slot) version is used when you don't want to do skill and emblem investments for Mistique as a sub-DPS. Additionally, this team is better than the usual Cosette (Main slot) setup if you need Mark triggers to activate the boss shill for Boss Blitz. ★ Key Notes: • Cursed Screech is the only potential that you need to max out for its Umbra DMG Taken debuff. • The other three priority potentials are just QoL potentials to improve Mistique's overall gameplay.

## Skill

None of Mistique's usable skills provide buff and/or debuff for the team to use and she doesn't deal damage on Main slot so there is no need to invest on any of her skills at all.

# Otoha

Otoha's skill build relies on the synergy of Ripple of Shadow , Evening Rai n , and Rain-Woven Sorrow to bombard a specific area with Sweeping Sky. This works best against enemies who are gathered in one place and bosses that are not mobile. ★ Key Notes: • Make sure that enemies are within the bombardment area of Sweeping Sky as this is the only way she can deal damage on this build. • Devouring Shadow provides a unique DEF PEN buff that requires Otoha to be in an all-Umbra team to work.

## Skill

Shattered Edge (Support Skill) is the only skill to prioritize for Otoha since both Ripple of Shadow and Evening Rain 's DMG scales based on this skill's level.

# Cosette

Cosette is the staple support for any Umbra team at the moment due to the consistent high Umbra DMG Taken debuff and ATK% buffs that she is able to provide. ★ Key Notes: • Phantom Mark is preferred over Abyssal Mark as it allows Cosette to be on field longer. • Dark: Mirage is a very good potential to take as the 4th option if used against a single enemy . If used on multiple enemies, it will negatively affect Dark: Afterglow and Dark: Demon Mark 's uptime. • Charge Efficiency from her emblem is only relevant if you have Nether Blaze on your Record.

## Skill

Abyssal Hunter (Support Skill) is the only skill that matters here as it scales the Umbra DMG Taken debuff inflicted based on the level of the skill.
