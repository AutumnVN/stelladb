# Teresa

Teresa takes on the Main slot as a support role of this team as she is able to provide ample amounts of ATK% buffs and Aqua DMG Taken debuffs to enemies that are hit by her Main Skill.

**★ Key Notes:**
• Casting another Main Skill requires at least 0.3s of delay in order to take advantage of **Stream: True Charged Strike**'s effects.
• Both **Cordiality** and **Muddy Engulf** will only activate if you hit the enemy.
• **Magic Sword Siphoning** is primarily a QoL potential that helps in making her buff and debuff uptimes be more consistent.
• Be wary about **Magic Sword Smack**'s ability to change break timings since its value can vary depending on your situation (e.g. cancelling boss phase changes/attack patterns and/or overall team investment)

## Skill

1/1+/1/1 (Main Skill only, otherwise not needed)

@skill(Limpid Stream) Upgrading **Limpid Stream (Main Skill)** allows Teresa's Surging Shield to absorb damage better. This upgrade is not mandatory. Only do it if you find yourself struggling to survive.

# Donna

Donna acts as the main DPS of the team, dishing out high burst damage

**★ Key Notes:**
• At least one level of **Chilling Onslaught** is required in order for **Aquatic Assault** to work.
• When it comes to comps with Donna in it, disc harmony levels are a better investment than maxxing out the last priority potentials.

## Skill

1/1/10/1 (Support Skill only)

@skill(Tide Threader) **Tide Threader (Support Skill)** is Donna's primary damage source through its innately high multipliers. Its level also directly scales the damage dealt by **Unleashed Arrow** and **Arrowfall Reckoning**.

# Suntide Willow

## Skill

1/1/1+/1+ (Support Skill > Ultimate)

@skill(Frolic in the Sea) @skill(Beach Warning) Prioritize upgrading **Frolic in the Sea (Support Skill)** first and **Beach Warning (Ultimate)** next since her Support Skill gives more buffs than her Ultimate.

Unfortunately, the buff scaling for both skills is a bit underwhelming compared to other Trekkers. Additionally, while her Support Skill does affect the damage scaling of **Flying Droplets** and **Flooding Shore**, her damage contribution remains to be minimal. Therefore, you can comfortably stop at Level 7 if you're low on resources.
