# Teresa

Teresa is the core support of the team as she is able to provide high Auto ATK and ATK% buffs through her potentials. ★ Key Notes: • Isn't it Awesome could stay at Lv. 1 for its 25% Attack Speed bonus until Shortcut Seeker is maxed out. • Isn't it Awesome is a high-risk, high-reward potential that needs you to maintain her shield throughout its entire duration (meaning avoiding taking any damage that can deplete the shield). • Hold On! is a nice optional potential to pick up if you have a surplus in potentials.

## Skill

Upgrading Billowing Tide (Support Skill) allows Teresa's Surging Shield to absorb damage better. This upgrade is not mandatory . Only do it if you find yourself getting hit often.

# Freesia

Freesia 's primary usage for this team is to provide buffs and debuff via Temporal Revival (Auto Attack buff) and Winter's Grip (Aqua DMG Taken debuff) respectively. ★ Key Notes: • She has higher buffing ceilings than Iris but is less consistent due to Temporal Revival having a 50-50 proc rate which you will need to re-stack from the start if the duration expires during combat. • You may take Guardian's Heart if there is a surplus in potentials.

## Skill

Freesia's Berserk state uses Auto Attacks but her damage output is minimal and negligible for this team. Thus, you don't need to invest on any skills and can safely use her for the buffing and debuffing potentials she provides.

# Iris

Iris is another support that fulfills the same role as Freesia. Contrary to popular belief, she performs competitively against Freesia when the average proc rate of Freesia's Temporal Revival is considered. Because Iris provides far more consistent buffing, Freesia’s pull value decreases specifically as a support for this team. ★ Key Notes: • Prioritize upgrading Bundle Sale and Sneaky Fisher over Forced Liquidation . • Both Raging Blizzard and Prolonged Winter are only used for their unique effect,

## Skill

Iris is mainly used for her valuable Potential kit and none of her skills scale buffs or debuffs so there is no need to waste resources on her.
