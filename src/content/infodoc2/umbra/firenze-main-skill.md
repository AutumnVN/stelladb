# Firenze

Firenze's Main Skill build focuses on dishing out sustained high skill damage through the synergy of the **Nimble Step** and **High Risk** potentials.

**★ Key Notes:**
• Focus on keeping **Nimble Step**'s domain active to consistently proc the additional damage from **High Risk**.
• Be mindful of Firenze's position when using Main Skill as it is prone to missing its hits due to its inability to be directionally adjusted.
• Do not let Firenze be staggered, otherwise, your rotation falls apart in terms of timing.
• **Place Your Bets** is a powerful potential that amplifies her Ultimate's damage output.
• Try to wait until Firenze has max stacks of **Flash Break** before casting her Main Skill or Ultimate.

## Skill

1/10/1/10 (Main Skill > Ultimate)

@skill(Elegant Parade) **Elegant Parade (Main Skill)** will be the main priority to upgrade as not only does it increase its own base multipliers but it also affects **Step Kicking** and **Nimble Step** due to their multipliers scaling based on its level.

@skill(Personal Tuition) **Personal Tuition (Ultimate)** is the next priority to upgrade as it offers high damage multipliers by default and does not result in a damage loss, especially if you have **Place Your Bets** in your Record.

# Cosette

Cosette is the staple support for any Umbra team at the moment due to the consistent high Umbra DMG Taken debuff and ATK% buffs that she is able to provide.

**★ Key Notes:**
• **Phantom Mark** is preferred over **Abyssal Mark** as it allows Cosette to be on field longer.
• **Dark: Mirage** is a very good potential to take as the 4th potential if used against a single enemy. If used on multiple enemies, it will negatively affect **Dark: Afterglow** and **Dark: Demon Mark**'s uptime.
• Charge Efficiency from her emblem is only relevant if you have **Nether Blaze** on your Record.

# Otoha - WS

Otoha's skill build relies on the synergy of **Ripple of Shadow**, **Evening Rain**, and **Rain-Woven Sorrow** to bombard a specific area with Sweeping Sky. This works best against enemies who are gathered in one place and bosses that are not mobile.

**★ Key Notes:**
• Make sure that enemies are within the bombardment area of Sweeping Sky as this is the only way she can deal damage and inflict debuffs on this build.
• **Devouring Shadow** provides a unique DEF PEN buff that requires Otoha to be in an all-Umbra team to work.

# Otoha - Laser

Otoha's laser build pales in comparison with her skill build when it comes to providing buffs and debuffs. However, what it brings is the ability to be used against bosses that are mobile.

**★ Key Notes:**
• **Solitary Lantern**, along with **Night-Walker**, are the main potentials that enable her to do damage.
• Having a +1 level of **Solitary Lantern** on your emblem is a big priority for this build as its Ultimate Crit Rate buff gets doubled at Lv 7.
• **Devouring Shadow** provides a unique DEF PEN buff that requires Otoha to be in an all-Umbra team to work.
• Ensure that her Support Skill is on cooldown before using her Ultimate to take advantage of the buff from **Without A Trace**.
• **Hollow Steps** synergizes with **Night-Walker** and **Solitary Lantern** as it consistently increase her personal Ultimate Crit Rate and Crit DMG.

# Mistique

If you don't have Otoha, Mistique can take her place as a debuffer with **Solar Absorption**. She can also transition to being a sub-DPS for the team if heavily invested.

**★ Key Notes:**
• **Malice** is required to activate the secondary effects of Petals of Blooming Mirage and Phantom of the Banquet's Melody Skill.
• **Solar Absorption** will be her first and foremost priority for this build since you rely on Firenze to do damage.
• **The Conjuring** must be maxed if you want Mistique's damage contribution to be higher because her damage output heavily relies on how much minions she can summon.
• The rest of the potentials are only relevant if you want her to be a sub-DPS.
• For emblem investment, only aim for **Solar Absorption** if you want her to be a pure debuffer.

# Coronis

If you do not have Otoha or Mistique, Coronis is the final alternative to take their place. Her role for the team is to provide ATK% and Umbra DMG Taken debuffs and help with depleting enemy Resilience with her **Medical Research** potential.

**★ Key Notes:**
• The 5% resilience depletion from **Medical Research** does not increase further with higher levels.

# Rotation

The goal of this team is to allow Firenze's Nimble Step potential to have permanent uptime as it allows High Risk to be proc'd consistently.

### General Rotation
__Firenze/Cosette/Otoha or Coronis__
1. Cosette Supp. Skill + Otoha/Coronis Supp. Skill
2. 1st Firenze Main Skill when Cosette skill hits 11s CD
3. 2nd Firenze Main Skill when Cosette skill hits 5s CD
*Note: This can be auto friendly if Firenze does not get staggered or miss any skill hits.

__without Mistique pre-casting__
1. Mistique Supp. Skill + Cosette Supp. Skill
2. 1st Firenze Main Skill when Cosette skill hits 11s CD
3. 2nd Firenze Main Skill when Cosette skill hits 5s CD

__with Mistique pre-casting__
1. Mistique Supp. Skill
2. Wait until Mistique's Supp. Skill CD reaches 2s
3. Cosette Supp. Skill
4. 1st Firenze Main Skill when Cosette skill hits 11s CD
5. Mistique Supp. Skill
6. 2nd Firenze Main Skill when Cosette skill hits 5s CD

### When to use Ultimate?
• Firenze: Ensure that Nimble Step is still active with around 3+ seconds left and Cosette is still on the field.
• Cosette (with Nether Blaze): Before using her skill.
• Otoha: When her skill CD is at 5-11s.
• Mistique: Whenever available as long as her skill is not cancelled.
• Coronis: Preferably when the enemy has resilience bar active.

### Tips
• If you have Wisteria Dream as Main Disc, use Firenze's skill (or any Umbra unit that spawns a minion like Mistique) until she has 3 stacks of Reverence if the Boss Blitz stage allows it. The Evernight Emberflies stage is a perfect example for this as it is not an instant battle against the boss.
