# Teresa

Teresa takes on the Main slot as a support role of this team as she is able to provide ample amounts of ATK% buffs and Aqua DMG Taken debuffs to enemies that are hit by her Main Skill.

**★ Key Notes:**
• Casting another Main Skill requires at least 0.3s of delay in order to take advantage of **Stream: True Charged Strike**'s effects.
• **Stream: Drip's Overturn** is mainly used to help Freesia charge her energy faster so she can help out as a secondary DPS earlier.
• Both **Cordiality** and **Muddy Engulf** will only activate if you hit the enemy.
• **Magic Sword Siphoning** is primarily a QoL potential that helps in making her buff and debuff uptimes be more consistent.

## Skill

1/1+/1/1 (Main Skill only, otherwise not needed)

@skill(Limpid Stream) Upgrading **Limpid Stream (Main Skill)** allows Teresa's Surging Shield to absorb damage better. This upgrade is not mandatory. Only do it if you find yourself struggling to survive.

# Freesia

Following the Patch 1.9 adjustments to **Ice Vortex**, Freesia's contribution now leans towards the Aqua DMG Taken debuff from **Winter's Grip** and **Guardian's Heart**'s team-wide Crit Rate buff.

**★ Key Notes:**
• The optional potentials below simply serves as filler potentials so she does not appear as often when you are doing Ascension.
• Only **Winter's Grip** and **Guardian's Heart** are relevant to her emblem investments if you don't want to cope into building her as a sub-DPS.

## Skill

1/1/1/1 (Not needed)

With the adjustments made to **Ice Vortex**, it's simply not worth it to invest into her as a sub-DPS for this team for single target boss content.

However, if you still want to do so, upgrade **Frost Bloom (Support Skill)** to max because **Localized Snowfall** scales based on its level.

### General Rotation

### When to use Ultimate?

• Teresa: Only if you have **Wave Breaker** and/or need the i-frame
• Donna: Between 2-8s remaining on her Supp. Skill cooldown
• Freesia: Best used if Supp. Skill is also available
