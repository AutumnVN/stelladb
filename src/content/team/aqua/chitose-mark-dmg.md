# Tilia

Of course Tilia is involved yet again when Mark DMG is involved thanks to **Blinding Beam**.

**★ Key Notes:**
• **Blinding Beam** is her most important potential for this team. If you don't have it maxed, reset.
• **Holy Inscription** enables the Mark DMG Taken debuff from **Blinding Beam** to be consistently active as long as the enemy is within the circle it spawns.
• **Renewed Spectrum** is taken to reduce the cooldown of her Support Skill.

## Skill

1/1/10/1 (Support Skill only)

@skill(Aegis Intervention) **Aegis Intervention (Support Skill)** provides an ATK% buff to the team when her skill deals damage. This buff scales according to the level of the skill and thus, it is beneficial to upgrade it.
